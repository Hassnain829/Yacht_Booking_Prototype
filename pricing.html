50% deposit

A 50% deposit secures your date. The balance is charged automatically 7 days before departure — or pay in full at checkout.

Cancellations

Full refund 30+ days out. 50% refund 14–29 days out. Inside 14 days, you receive a credit valid for 12 months.

Weather guarantee

If the captain cancels for weather, reschedule free or take a non-expiring credit for 100% of what you paid.

Crew gratuity

Gratuity is optional and goes 100% to your captain and crew. 18–20% is customary for great service.
