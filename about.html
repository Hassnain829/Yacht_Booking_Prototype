Our story

From one sailboat to Chicago's favorite charter fleet

Chicago Yacht Booking began with a single sailboat at Belmont Harbor and a captain who loved showing friends the skyline from the lake. Word spread, the calendar filled, and we realized something: people wanted the experience, but hated the hassle.

So we built the company we'd want to book with. Live availability. Clear, all-in pricing with the captain already included. A team that picks up the phone. Today we run a hand-picked fleet across four Chicago harbors — and every charter still gets the same care as that very first sail.
